Easy Dumplings

Description

Dumplings are delicious bite-sized pieces of dough filled with a flavorful mixture of meat and vegetables. They are soft and tender on the inside, with a lightly crispy or golden exterior when pan-fried. Served with soy sauce, chili oil, or a dipping sauce, dumplings make a tasty appetizer, snack, or main meal.

Ingredients

  • 250 g all-purpose flour
  • 180 ml warm water
  • 250 g ground pork, chicken, or beef
  • 1 cup finely chopped cabbage
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ tsp grated ginger
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Make the dough: Mix flour and salt. Gradually add warm water and knead for about 5–7 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Make the filling: Mix the meat, cabbage, green onions, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and pepper.
  3. Roll the wrappers: Divide dough into small balls. Roll each into a thin circle, roughly 8–10 cm wide.
  4. Fill: Place about 1 tablespoon of filling in the center. Fold the wrapper over and firmly pinch the edges closed.
  5. Pan-fry: Heat a little oil, place dumplings flat-side down, and cook until golden. Add about ½ cup water, cover, and steam for 6–8 minutes.
  6. Serve with soy sauce, chili oil, or a mixture of soy sauce + rice vinegar.
