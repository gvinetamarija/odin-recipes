Pancakes

Description

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are quick to make and perfect for a weekend breakfast. They only need a few pantry staples and can be topped with fruit, syrup, or butter.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 250 ml milk
  • 125g flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • A little butter or oil for the pan

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs and milk together.
  2. Add flour, sugar, and salt. Whisk until smooth.
  3. Stir in the melted butter.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour in a small amount of batter and swirl the pan to make a very thin layer.
  6. Cook for about 1 minute, flip, and cook another 20–30 seconds.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter.
