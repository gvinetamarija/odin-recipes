Simple Vegetable Soup

Description

This simple vegetable soup is warm, comforting, and full of fresh vegetables. It is made with carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, and tomatoes cooked in a tasty broth with herbs. It is easy to prepare, healthy, and perfect for a cozy lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g)
  • 1 liter vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 tsp dried herbs
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add the carrots, potatoes, and celery. Cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes, broth, and dried herbs.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat.
  6. Simmer for 15–20 minutes, until the vegetables are soft.
  7. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Serve hot.
